THE FOUR WALLS

How your data is protected

01 · READ-ONLY

We can look. We can never touch.

Every connection is read-only. Watchdogs audit transactions — they cannot move money, edit records, or change your books. Not restricted from it. Incapable of it.

02 · BANK-GRADE CONNECTIONS

Your bank credentials never touch us

Bank feeds connect through Plaid, the same secure connection layer used by leading financial apps. You authenticate with your bank directly. We never see or store your banking login.

03 · WALLED-OFF DATA

Your data is yours. Only yours.

Every member's data is isolated at the database level with row-level security. No member can ever see another member's data — the wall is enforced by the database itself, not by good intentions.

04 · LOGGED & ENCRYPTED

Every action leaves a trail

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Credentials live in encrypted storage, never in code. Watchdog activity is logged, so there's always a record of what was read and when.

WHAT WE WILL NEVER DO

Three promises, in writing.

Never move your money

No payment rails, no transfer permissions, no exceptions. Findings come to you — you act on them.

Never sell your data

Your financial data exists for one purpose: finding your leaks. It is never sold, shared, or used to train anything for anyone else.

Never hold you hostage

Disconnect any time. Your connections revoke instantly, and your data goes with you.
